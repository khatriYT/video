What would happen if all mosquitoes disappeared? Tonight at midnight, every mosquito on Earth drops dead: all 3,500 species. No more bites, no more malaria. Sounds perfect… but scientists don't agree on what comes next.

Pip follows the ripple effects from backyard ponds to the Arctic tundra, from a rare orchid that depends on mosquitoes to the hospitals where malaria kills around 600,000 people a year. Along the way: how a mosquito finds you in the dark, 99-million-year-old mosquitoes trapped in amber, the bat myth that isn't quite true, the Panama Canal, the failed 1955–1969 plan to wipe out malaria, and the new science (Wolbachia mosquitoes and gene drives) that could let us remove only the dangerous species.
