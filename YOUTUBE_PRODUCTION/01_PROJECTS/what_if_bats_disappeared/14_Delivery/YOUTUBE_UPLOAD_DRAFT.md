## Description
A fungus has already wiped out millions of North American bats, so we don't have to imagine a world without them. In this episode of The Disappearance Files we follow what happened next:
- farmers used more insecticide;
- a study in *Science* linked the bat die-off to more infant deaths;
- and we see what bats do every night as pest controllers, pollinators (agave, durian) and forest planters.
