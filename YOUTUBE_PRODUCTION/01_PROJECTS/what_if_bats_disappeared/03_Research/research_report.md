# Research Report: What If All Bats Disappeared?

- **Compiled:** 2026-11-26.
- **Sources:** `source_library.md` (S01–S21).
- **Claims:** `claim_checklist.csv` (C01–C32).

## The core story

A fungus has already removed most of the bats from large parts of eastern North America. That makes this topic unusual: we don't have to model a world with fewer bats, because a real one exists. The hook is a 2024 *Science* paper (S01). It used the county-by-county spread of white-nose syndrome as a natural experiment. Where bats collapsed:
- farmers used about 31% more insecticide;
- infant deaths from internal causes rose about 7.9%.

That works out to an estimated 1,334 extra infant deaths in 245 counties between 2006 and 2017.

## What is solid

| Topic | Finding | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | White-nose syndrome (WNS) was first photographed in Howes Cave, New York, in February 2006 | S02 |
| Spread | Confirmed in more than 40 US states and parts of Canada | S04 |
| Declines | More than 90% for three species in under 10 years | S03 |
| Mechanism | Infection is linked to frequent arousals from hibernation, and arousals burn fat | S06 |
| Pest-control value | More than $3.7 bn a year to North American agriculture, with a wide range | S07 |
| Pest control in the field | Texas cotton valued at about $741k a year in 8 counties (S08). Exclosure experiment in Illinois corn: nearly 60% more larvae and 56% more kernel damage without bats (S09) | S08, S09 |
| Pollination | More than 500 plant species are bat-pollinated (S13). The examples (agave, durian) are documented by researchers | S13, S14, S15 |
| Seed dispersal | Bats carry the seeds of pioneer plants into cleared land | S16 |
| Culling backfires | Culling backfired in two documented cases: vampire bats in Peru and fruit bats at Kitaka Mine in Uganda | S17, S18 |
| Hope | Some little brown bat colonies are persisting (S19). Turbine curtailment cuts bat deaths by about 60% (S20) | S19, S20 |

## Sensitive content plan

- **Infant mortality:** shown only as data. There are no images of babies, illness or grief.
- **Dead bats:** not shown. Declines are shown as empty roosts and data.
- **Disease:** framed with evidence. The message is "leave bats alone", not "bats are dangerous".
