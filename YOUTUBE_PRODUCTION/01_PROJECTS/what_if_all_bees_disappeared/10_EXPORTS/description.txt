What would happen if all bees disappeared? Tomorrow morning, every bee on Earth is gone: all 20,000 species. Would we starve in four years, like the famous "Einstein quote" says? (He never said it.) The truth is stranger, and it starts in your supermarket.

Pip finds out what really depends on bees: 87 of the world's 115 leading crops, California's almond bloom that needs about 2.7 million rented hives, farmers in China pollinating apple trees by hand, the bumblebees that "buzz" tomatoes, and the cows, clothes and cooking oil you'd never connect to bees. Plus why many bees really are in trouble, and what you can do about it.
