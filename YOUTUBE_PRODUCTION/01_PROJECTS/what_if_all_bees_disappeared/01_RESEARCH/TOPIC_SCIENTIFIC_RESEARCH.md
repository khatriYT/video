# Scientific Research: What Would Happen If All Bees Disappeared?

Researched 2026-11-23.

**Tags:** [FACT] established · [INTERP] interpretation · [HYPO] hypothetical prediction · [UNCERTAIN] open or conflicting

## 1. Bee diversity
- **[FACT]** About **20,000 described bee species**.
  - Most are solitary and don't make honey. The share is given as 77–95% depending on source and definition, so the script says "most".
  - Honeybees (*Apis*) are about 12 species; *Apis mellifera* is the main managed one.
  - Sources: [WWF](https://www.worldwildlife.org/stories/five-myths-about-bees-the-truth-about-these-remarkable-insects), [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/theres-much-more-to-bees-than-queens-honey-and-hives-41380)
- **[FACT]** Bumblebees fly in cool weather. Mason bees build mud partitions. Leafcutter bees line nests with cut leaf discs. The smallest bees (e.g. *Perdita minima*) are about 2 mm long.
- **[UNCERTAIN]** *Melittosphex burmensis*, about 100 Ma in amber, was once called the "oldest bee". **Later work rejected it as a bee**, so the script avoids "oldest bee fossil" claims. Bees are thought to have evolved from predatory wasps. The script says "something like a hundred million years" of partnership with flowers.

## 2. The Einstein quote
- **[FACT]** "If the bee disappeared off the face of the earth, man would only have four years of life left" has **no evidence of coming from Einstein**. It is listed as "probably not by Einstein" in *The Ultimate Quotable Einstein* and debunked by Quote Investigator and Snopes. Sources: [Quote Investigator](https://quoteinvestigator.com/?p=7108), [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/quotes/einstein/bees.asp)

## 3. Pollination
- **[FACT]** About 87.5% of flowering plant species are animal-pollinated (Ollerton, Winfree & Tarrant 2011, *Oikos*). A 2023 study (*National Science Review*) estimates about 90% of species. Script: "nearly nine in ten". [EurekAlert 2023](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1000062)
- **[FACT]** Bees carry branched (plumose) hairs, and honeybees and bumblebees carry pollen baskets (corbiculae). Bees see ultraviolet, and many petals have UV nectar guides.
- **[FACT]** Buzz pollination (sonication): bumblebees and many other bees vibrate their flight muscles to release pollen from poricidal anthers (tomato, potato, eggplant, blueberry). **Honeybees can't buzz-pollinate.** [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thesalt/2016/07/19/486501293/watch-the-secret-buzz-only-bumblebees-know-to-unlock-our-favorite-crops)
- **[FACT]** Wheat, rice and maize are wind-pollinated or self-pollinated. Together they provide roughly half of human dietary energy (FAO commonly cited; script: "around half").

## 4. Crops
- **[FACT]** Klein et al. 2007 (*Proc. R. Soc. B* 274:303):
  - **87 of 115 leading global crops** benefit to some degree from animal pollination.
  - By volume, 35% of production comes from pollinator-dependent crops and 60% from crops that don't depend on them.
  - The 87/115 figure counts crop types, not a share of food.
  - [PMC1702377](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1702377/)
- **[FACT]** Cocoa is pollinated mainly by ceratopogonid midges, not bees. This links back to episode 1.
- **[FACT]** Alfalfa *seed* production relies on bees (alfalfa leafcutting bee, alkali bee). The hay feeds livestock, including dairy cattle. [USDA ARS](https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/logan-ut/pollinating-insect-biology-management-systematics-research/docs/alfalfa-leafcutting-bee-alcb/)
- **[FACT]** California almonds: about 1.4 million acres. About **2.7 million colonies** were needed for the 2024 bloom (estimated 2.8 M for 2025), most trucked in from other states. [farmdoc daily 2025](https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/2025/02/where-have-all-the-honey-bees-gone-to-california-almond-orchards.html)

## 5. Health
- **[FACT, model]** Smith et al. 2015, *The Lancet* (386:1964):
  - Complete loss of animal pollinators: about **1.42 million additional deaths per year**, from non-communicable and malnutrition-related disease. It also projects 71 M people newly vitamin A deficient and 173 M newly folate deficient, in low-income countries.
  - A 50% loss gives about 700,000 extra deaths.
  - **This applies to all animal pollinators, not bees alone.** The script says so explicitly.
  - [Harvard PDF](https://scholar.harvard.edu/sites/scholar.harvard.edu/files/mattsmith/files/lancet_smith_et_al_2015.pdf)

## 6. Hand pollination
- **[FACT]** Maoxian County and nearby Hanyuan, Sichuan, China: heavy pesticide use reduced bees, and apples and pears were hand-pollinated (100% of Maoxian apples in 2001 fieldwork). A study estimated bee pollination could be about **8× cheaper** than hand pollination. [Partap & Ya, Mountain Research and Development 2012](https://complete.bioone.org/journals/Mountain-Research-and-Development/volume-32/issue-2/MRD-JOURNAL-D-11-00108.1/The-Human-Pollinators-of-Fruit-Crops-in-Maoxian-County-Sichuan/10.1659/MRD-JOURNAL-D-11-00108.1.full)
- **[FACT]** Robotic pollinators (e.g. Harvard RoboBee, first flight 2013, tethered) and drone prototypes exist; none is a practical large-scale replacement. [INTERP]

## 7. Declines
- **[FACT]** Drivers, per the IPBES 2016 pollinator assessment: habitat loss, pesticides (including neonicotinoids), parasites and pathogens (Varroa mite and the viruses it spreads), and climate change. Multiple interacting stressors.
- **[FACT]** In April 2018 the EU banned all outdoor uses of clothianidin, imidacloprid and thiamethoxam.
- **[INTERP]** The relative weight of each driver is debated.

## 8. Solutions
- **[FACT]** Native flower plantings, wildflower strips, reduced pesticide use, and nesting habitat (bare ground, hollow stems) support wild bees.
- **[INTERP]** Wild insects improve fruit set beyond what honeybees provide (Garibaldi et al. 2013, *Science*), so the script says flower strips "often" help.

## Script framing rules for this episode
- Say "**animal pollinators**" when citing the Lancet model.
- "Would we starve?" → **no mass starvation**. Staple grains are wind-pollinated, but diets get worse and many people would die from poorer nutrition (model).
- Don't claim honey lasts forever, and don't repeat the "oldest bee fossil" claim.

## 9. Additions (v2 of script)
- **[FACT]** A honeybee colony holds tens of thousands of workers, about 20,000–60,000 in summer. The on-screen "50,000" is a typical summer figure. The workers are daughters of one queen.
- **[FACT]** The waggle dance encodes direction (the angle relative to vertical represents the angle to the sun) and distance (the waggle duration). Karl von Frisch, Nobel Prize 1973.
- **[FACT/INTERP]** A jar or pound of honey takes visits to millions of flowers. The commonly cited figure of about 2 million flowers per pound is order-of-magnitude, so the script says "millions".
- **[FACT]** Rader et al. 2016, *PNAS* 113:146: non-bee insects make about 39% of visits to crop flowers (25–50% range). They are less effective per visit but make more visits, so their overall service is similar to bees.
- **[FACT]** California almond bloom is often described as the largest managed pollination event in the world.
- **[INTERP]** Bees are thought to have evolved from predatory (apoid) wasps. The "~100 million years" figure is approximate.
- **[FACT]** Colony collapse disorder (CCD) was reported in the US in late 2006: adult workers absent, queen, brood and food stores present. Migratory beekeepers move hives from almonds to other crops (apples, cherries, blueberries). Greenhouse tomato growers replaced electric vibrators with commercial bumblebee colonies.
