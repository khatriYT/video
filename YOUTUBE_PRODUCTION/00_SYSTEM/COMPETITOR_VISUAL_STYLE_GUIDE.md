## PART A: How the competitor looks

### A.1 Illustration style
- 2D, hand-drawn, flat-coloured cartoon. It is not 3D and not clean geometric vector art.
- Outlines are near-black or dark brown. They are thick (about 6–8 px at 1080p) and slightly irregular, which gives a hand-inked feel.
- Fills are flat with very soft shading: a gentle gradient or a darker shape for shadow. A subtle paper or grain texture shows in some backgrounds.
- The overall look is minimalist characters on fairly detailed backgrounds.

### A.2 Characters
- **Head:** a large plain circle, off-white for living people and grey-green for zombies. It is about 1/3 of the total body height.
- **Eyes:** two small black dots. Zombies have dark rings around their eyes.
- **Mouth:** a small "o" for shock, a simple curve for a smile, a flat line for worry. There is no nose and almost never eyebrows.
- **Body:** a single thick black line for the torso and limbs (stick figure). Clothing is a flat **rectangle or trapezoid** laid over the torso: apron, hoodie, vest or dress.
- **Hands:** often oversized white cartoon gloves with 4 fingers, used for gestures and for zombie hands reaching in.
- **Identifiers:** hats and accessories. The hero wears a **red baseball cap and blue or teal clothes**. Others are told apart by hair shape, glasses, lanyards and hats.
- **Expressions:** almost entirely carried by the mouth shape plus a few sweat drops or motion lines.

### A.3 Backgrounds and objects
- Interiors drawn in **one-point perspective** (supermarket aisles, corridors, plane cabins) to give depth behind flat characters.
- Objects are simplified but recognisable (shelves of colourful blocks, doors, lockers, school buses) with the same ink outline.
- Exteriors use layered flat silhouettes: city skylines, tree lines, hills.

### A.4 Colour palette
- Muted, slightly desaturated mid-tones: beige floors, grey-blue walls, faded greens.
- Night scenes are deep navy or charcoal lit by **one warm or cool light source** (candle, flashlight, moon).
- The red cap is the saturated accent that leads the eye.

### A.5 Lighting
- Ambient flat lighting plus gradient vignettes. Light sources are drawn as soft radial glows.

### A.6 Composition and camera
- The hero is placed on a vertical third and the threat on the opposite side.
- Shot types: close-ups (head plus shoulders, often oversized), medium shots (full stick figure) and wide crowd shots.
- The eye-level camera is predominant.

### A.7 Image format
- 16:9 at 1280×720 or larger, probably produced at 1920×1080.

### A.8 Animation techniques [INFERRED, NOT VERIFIED]
These are typical of this art style (cut-out or "puppet" 2D animation in After Effects, Moho or similar).

- Mostly **held poses** with slow camera push-ins or pans over still illustrations.
- Limb rotations from joints (arm raise, head tilt), blinks, and mouth-shape swaps.
- Crowds animated by simple bobbing or swaying loops.
- Light flicker and particle effects (rain, snow, fog breath).
- Hard cuts or quick dissolves between shots, each shot lasting about 3–8 s.

### A.9 What we must NOT copy
- The red-cap hero or the grey-green zombie designs.
- Their thumbnail layouts one-to-one, including the white diagonal-bar DAY split with a red second number.
- Their episode titles.

## PART B: OUR HOUSE STYLE (working name "The Disappearance Files")

### B.1 Style summary (paste this into every image prompt)
> 2D hand-drawn cartoon illustration, flat colours with soft simple shading, thick slightly wobbly dark-brown ink outlines (#2B2420), stick-figure characters with large round heads and dot eyes, simplified but recognisable naturalist environments, muted earthy palette with one saturated accent, subtle paper grain, 16:9, 1920×1080, no text unless specified.

### B.2 Mascot: "Pip", the field naturalist
| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Head | Circle, radius about 60 px at medium shot. Warm off-white #F6F0E2. 7 px outline. |
| Glasses | **Round wire glasses** (dark brown rims around the dot eyes). This is our main face identifier. |
| Hat | **Mustard-yellow bucket hat** (#F2B33D) with a darker band (#C98A1E). |
| Eyes / mouth | Black dot eyes. Mouth shapes: smile, "o", flat, frown. Blinks every 3–5 s. |
| Body | Single thick dark-brown line torso. **Olive field vest** (#6E8B3D) as a rounded trapezoid with a chest pocket. |
| Limbs | Dark-brown lines, 7 px. Round mitten hands in head colour. |
| Legs / shoes | Two lines. Brown boots, as small dark rounded rectangles. |
| Prop | Brown satchel (#8A5A35) on a cross-body strap (optional). |

### B.3 Supporting cast
These are generic roles, varied every episode. Skin tones vary across characters, which sets us apart from the competitor's all-white heads.

- **Scientist A:** grey hair tuft, white lab coat, light skin tone #F3D9C0.
- **Scientist B:** black hair in a high bun, white lab coat, deep skin tone #8D5A3B.
- **Parent and child:** headscarf or curly hair, warm clothing colours.

### B.4 Creature design: the mosquito
- Slim brown-grey body with **black-and-white banded abdomen**, inspired by Aedes.
- Big round eyes made of two white dots with black pupils. Mosquitoes are the only characters with "big eyes", which makes them read as cute and expressive.
- Thin long legs (6). A long straight proboscis. Two translucent pale-blue oval wings (#CFE7F2, 60% opacity).
- In motion, the wings flutter on alternating frames and the body follows a figure-8 hover path.

### B.5 Palettes

### B.6 Composition and camera
- **Rule of thirds.** Pip stands on one third, the subject of the narration on the other.
- **Shots:**
  - ECU (close-up of an object or bite)
  - MCU (head plus vest)
  - MS (full figure)
  - WS (environment)
  - Globe / map
- Keep the important action inside the central 80% (title-safe area).

### B.7 Animation rules
- Every clip gets subtle **line boil**: the outline wobble changes 8 times per second. This gives a hand-drawn feel.
- **Camera:** slow push-in or pull-out of up to 8% scale, or a pan. Use eased movement and never more than one camera move per shot.
- **Character:** blink, breathing bob, 1–2 meaningful joint moves (arm, head tilt, hat drop). No full walk cycles unless the narration needs one.
- **Creatures:** looping hovering or crawling.
- **Shot length:** the narration segment plus about 0.3–0.6 s of breathing room, usually 3–8 s.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts by default. Use a 0.25 s dissolve for a time jump or change of location.

### B.8 Title and thumbnail text
- Font: Inter Display Black (or any heavy rounded sans-serif).
- Letters: cream #FFF6E0 with a 10–14 px dark-brown outline (#2B2420). One accent word in mustard or alarm red.

### B.9 Consistency checklist (QC every image)
- [ ] Outline colour and weight match.
- [ ] Pip has a mustard bucket hat, round glasses and an olive vest.
- [ ] Palette taken from B.5.
- [ ] Image is 16:9, 1920×1080.
- [ ] No unwanted text, no extra limbs, no 3D shading.
- [ ] The subject matches the narration.
