# Reference Visual Style Bible: Hardy Humans (wqMaepNXeBc)

This is a description of **their** look, to be used as production guidance only.

- Every observation comes from 225 storyboard samples (320×180).
- Colours marked [MEASURED] come from compressed previews, so treat them as ±10% approximations.
- What we must not copy is listed in §7.
- Our own house style lives in `00_SYSTEM/STYLE_BIBLE_v2_DRAFT.md`.

## 1. Layer model [VERIFIED]

```
Layer 4  On-screen text: caption word (bottom-centre), HUD stamp (top-left), title cards, number call-outs
Layer 3  Flat graphics: infographic cards, maps, floating objects (thermometer, pot, checklist)
Layer 2  Flat vector characters: round heads, simple bodies, black mitten hands, dog companion
Layer 1  Painted environment plate: detailed, painterly, mood-lit; defocused for close-ups
```

## 2. Characters [VERIFIED]

| Element | Observation |
|---|---|
| Head | Large plain circle, about 1/3 of body height in medium shots. Off-white with a faint grey shade on one side. Thin dark outline |
| Face | Two black dot eyes. Mouth is a short line, small arc or "o". Occasional eyebrow lines for worry. No nose or ears |
| Body | Simple flat clothing silhouettes: a puffer jacket with horizontal quilting lines, trousers, boots |
| Hands | Black rounded mittens. Used heavily in POV shots |
| Hero identifiers | Mustard-yellow puffer jacket, red scarf. Later a beanie, headset or backpack depending on the chapter |
| Supporting cast | Same head template, told apart by hair (grey bun, brown bob, blonde bun, beard), glasses, headsets and coat colours (green, purple, grey, navy) |
| Dog companion | A tan beagle type with white chest and blue collar. More naturalistic shading than the humans. Expressive ears and brows |
| Historical figures | Same template in period clothes, or **translucent "ghost" overlays** (the Norse settlers at 28:16) |
| Scale in frame | Wide shots: 5–20% of frame height. Close-ups: head fills 35–55% of frame height |
| Name labels | A tilted luggage tag with a pin icon and the name (Olena, Pavel), bottom-right, on a character's first close-up |

## 3. Environment plates [VERIFIED unless marked]

- **Style:** painterly semi-realism, best described as "illustrated storybook realism".
  - Soft global light, rich ambient occlusion and dense micro-detail: bricks, fire escapes, signs, litter, cracks, plants.
  - Likely AI-generated [INFERRED].
- **Recurring locations (sample counts):**

  | Location | Samples |
  |---|---|
  | Street | 18 |
  | Rooftop with water tower | 16 |
  | Radio room | 6 |
  | Arctic town | 6 |
  | Airport runway | 5 |
  | Cockpit | 5 |
  | Sunrise stairs | 5 |
  | Subway car | 4 |

- **Continuity:**
  - Locations keep their landmarks: the water tower, satellite dish, lawn chair and parapet on the rooftop; the taxi, bus and bare tree on the street.
  - Season, weather, wear and vegetation change between visits.
- **Perspective:**
  - Many plates use strong one-point perspective: subway car, bridge, highway, vault tunnel.
  - Others are eye-level street views or wide establishing shots.
- **Composition:** plates leave a clear "stage" area, usually lower-centre or lower-third, for flat characters to stand on.
- **Abandonment language:** open car doors, scattered bags, overgrowth, rust, snow drifts, broken windows, collapsed structures.
- **Flashback and historical plates** use the same painterly style with period content. A sepia treatment appears for 1942.

## 4. Graphics language [VERIFIED; colours MEASURED]

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Dark card background | Near-black navy, median #0D1019 (range #080F14–#101526) |
| Light card background | Cream / paper (≈ #E9E2D0 visually; compressed samples read darker) |
| Accent red (title numbers, key figures) | **#C0433C** (consistent across 7 title cards) |
| Secondary accents | Amber/gold for highlighted words; green ticks; muted teal or green land on maps |
| Icons | Flat, no outline or a thin outline; stick-figure pictograms; simple planes, buildings, thermometers, batteries |
| Maps | Dark navy ocean (#293140 median), desaturated green or grey land, white labels, dashed routes with small plane icons, range rings |
| Density | One idea per card; 1–3 labels; big numerals (e.g. "50,000,000", "-320°F", "4,000+") |

## 5. Typography [VERIFIED]

| Use | Style |
|---|---|
| Caption word | One word at a time, ALL CAPS, bold condensed sans-serif, white with dark outline/shadow, centred at about 88% of frame height |
| Title card | "DAY" in white, number in red, heavy geometric sans-serif, with a small spaced subtitle ("THE TUNNELS") underneath |
| HUD stamp | Small rounded rectangle, top-left: `DAY N · H:MM AM · NN°F` |
| Graphic labels | Clean geometric sans-serif, white or red, sentence or all caps |

## 6. Colour and mood [VERIFIED; MEASURED where marked]

| Setting | Colour treatment |
|---|---|
| Day plates | Warm (orange-brown brick, yellow taxis, golden light) against cool blue skies and shadows |
| Night plates | Deep blue/indigo base with warm practicals |
| Arctic act | Cooler palette: white, pale blue, violet dusk, orange sun |

## 7. What we must NOT copy

- The round white-head hero in a mustard puffer jacket and red scarf, the beagle companion, or any named character (Hardy, Bagel, Ruth, Signe…).
- Their exact title-card design (white DAY + red number + spaced subtitle) or the HUD stamp layout one-to-one.
- Their thumbnail layout (diagonal DAY 1 / DAY N split with a red second number).
- Any frames, plates or graphics, and their scripts or story beats.

Use the **principles** instead:
- layered plates plus flat graphics;
- evolving recurring locations;
- one fact per card;
- time-stamped chapters.
